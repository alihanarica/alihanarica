Receivables agent: a field prototype

Which overdue invoices should the collections team work on today, why, and what should they do? With the value case and pilot criteria for a first customer meeting.

Read this first. This is a one-day prototype by Alihan Arıca, built with AI coding tools to show how I prepare a value conversation. It is not an SAP product and is not connected to any system. The company, customers and figures are invented. SAP offers its own Joule agents for receivables; a prototype like this helps a customer see the value in their own terms before a pilot.

Today the team builds its daily call list by hand from an overdue report, mostly by amount. Large but safe customers get attention; smaller customers with worsening habits slip through.

2. How the agent works

ReadOpen invoices, payment history, credit limits and open disputes.
ScoreEach invoice gets a priority from five factors. Every score shows its reasons.
ProposeOne next action per invoice and a ready reminder in Turkish.
ApproveA collector approves or changes each action. Nothing is sent without a person.

This is the method I used in AI sales work: customer, seller and partner agree the acceptance and ROI criteria at the start. Proofs of concept ran about 30 to 40 percent shorter.
